# 030825

I've always tried to be attentive. To pay attention to my surroundings. It's a skill that requires time to master, a never ending learning path. This attentiveness has shaped my understanding of the world.

<!-- more -->

Experiences refine our personality, one can never entirely go beyond the boundaries of that baseline. I had the freedom to explore ideas and possibilities, yet my shy personality and anxieties always made me feel that opportunities were missed. Despite this, life finds its way to put you on the right path, even if it’s not the one initially envisioned.

A constant tension exists in not letting coincidence hinder understanding. I had to re-frame my ideas, judge my perceptions to improve. From a young age, one of the challenges I faced was seeing things in a different shade; I knew it was out there, but I didn't always have the words to convey it effectively. This led me to seek alternative ways of understanding and expressing myself.

There's always a path of least resistance, no matter the topic. Walking that path could lead to fascinating discoveries, though not without its challenges. I faced rejection, dismissal and plain derision. Given my quiet nature and hardship to make myself understand, words often failed to convey my findings. However, these challenges only strengthened my resolve.

Pressure and impositions provide a test to convictions. Silence and observation became my framework. Actions speak louder than words, there's always a way to make your intentions known. My mind will not be subverted; I will remain true to my values.

Looking around I can see power struggles and influence. Times may change, but values like respect, cooperation and selflessness are universals that transcend time and frontiers. Yet our interactions are often measured, hindering trust by not allowing ourselves to be truly open.

I spend too much time alone, what others consider common, for me is extraordinary. While I can be awkward, this solitude has also allowed me to enjoy beauty in little details, appreciating the nuances that others might overlook.

My interactions with others often felt like defusing a bomb, the tendency to overthink mixed with the desire to make things right putting me on edge. It's not without effort that I managed to break the avoidance pattern. I focused on being present, looking at my surroundings to see what needs to be done and how I could be of help with the little I have. This shift in focus helped me to overcome my anxieties and engage more fully with the world. 

I challenged my shyness and awkwardness; I'm far from where I want to be, but I've managed to break that cycle. This has lead me to unexpected situations, to try without expectations, to be as authentic as I can. Did my actions really lead me forward?

I walk, move forward, but I can't see any progress, the path I choose is a difficult one. I want my interactions to be organic, without pressure or expectations. This often goes against personal desires, in particular when feelings that I thought will never be for me emerge and linger. I can't force anyone to stay in my life; I respect their decisions, knowing that I can't meet anyone's expectations. I can only leave an invitation to my life, with the hope of being accepted for who I am. 

The idea of trying to convince to be chosen is not for me; it goes against my values. This is what I am. I won't expect anyone to remember or come back for me. In any case, nothing would make happier than being accepted for who I am. 

I'm worried that my words and body language don't convey my true intentions. My only desire is to create a space were people can be themselves, share their vision, help me learn how to enrich my life with different perspectives. I could be present and offer my hand if they so wish to take it.

I want to know what makes someone 'tick', find their passion, I could listen for countless hours, enthralled. It connects me with their experiences, helping me understand the vision behind it, the inner truth of a person. This desire for connection is a driving force in my life.

When it's meant to be, there's no escape, no hidden logic behind; it happens in a natural way. I've met wonderful people, life has found its way to give me the chance to prove that my convictions weren't wrong. The past may have been rough, full of missed opportunities, rejections and failures; yet there's always hope to make things right. If I hadn't chosen this way of life I would've never met them, shared life, even if it were a fleeting moment. For that I'm grateful. Those will be the best memories of my life.
